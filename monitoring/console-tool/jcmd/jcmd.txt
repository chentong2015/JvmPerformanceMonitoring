TODO. jcmd: Java运行时信息诊断和输出工具
https://docs.oracle.com/en/java/javase/17/docs/specs/man/jcmd.html

> jcmd 列出当前机器上Java进程

> jcmd pid VM.version

> jcmd pid VM.system_properties 输出系统属性 => 等效于jinfo

> jcmd pid Thread.print    输出线程的运行状态 => 等效于jstack

> jcmd pid GC.class_histogram 输出加载的类的内存情况

> jcmd pid GC.heap_dump C:/temp/myHeapDumpName.hprof 输出Heap Dump到文件
